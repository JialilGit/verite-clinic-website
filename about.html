About Our CEO

Discover the vision and expertise that drives VÉRITÉ DERMA CLINIC to redefine luxury aesthetic experiences.

Dr. Vivian Vance

Founder and lead aesthetician of VÉRITÉ DERMA CLINIC, Dr. Vivian Vance brings years of dedicated expertise in luxury beauty treatments to every client experience. Her journey began with a passion for enhancing natural beauty through meticulous attention to detail and artistic precision.

Her approach combines advanced technical skills with an innate understanding of facial aesthetics, ensuring each treatment is tailored to the individual's unique features and aspirations. Dr. Vance continuously pursues education in the latest techniques and technologies to maintain the highest standards of care.

At VÉRITÉ DERMA CLINIC, we believe that true luxury lies in the personalized experience — where every detail is considered, every comfort is provided, and every result exceeds expectations. Our commitment to excellence is reflected in our serene environment, premium products, and unwavering dedication to client satisfaction.

Our mission is to create a sanctuary where beauty is celebrated as an art form, and each visitor leaves feeling confident, rejuvenated, and beautifully themselves.

VÉRITÉ

The Vérité Brand is named after its founder and Medical Director, Dr. Vivian Vance, who established the clinic alongside an elite team of aesthetic specialists. Built on a relentless pursuit of innovation, safety, and clinical excellence, Vérité delivers high-end dermatological services tailored to modern lifestyle needs.
